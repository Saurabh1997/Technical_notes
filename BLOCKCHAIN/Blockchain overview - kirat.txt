blockchain has 3 types now ----
Layer 0 blockchain - Polkadot, cosmos. lets us build own blockchain. 
layer 1 blockchain - bitcoin, Solana, ethereum. where we can do crypto transactions.. build dapps on top of it.
layer 2 blockchain - polygon, optimism, arbitium. took different approach to scale blockchain. they are like smaller blockchains of their own. side roads of the main blockchain. related to ethereum. 

Usecases of blockchain - 
1. Wallet App. 
	Solana - phantom and backpack - multichain wallet. 
	Ethereum - Metamask & wallet connect. 
2. Decentralized exchange, decentralized orderbooks - swapping fees. which actually does the swapping. 
	Solana - Orca, meteora, jupiter. can build own frontends to do swapping. 
3. NFTs 
4. centralized exchanges - binance, coindcx, coinbase, wazirx. 

Roadmap - 
70% - bash/ terminal, MERN, RPC, deploying to prod. 
30% - smart contracst. 	Eth - solidy, solana - rust.
