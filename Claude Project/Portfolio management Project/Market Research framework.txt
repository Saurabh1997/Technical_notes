# Quantitative Investment and Market Research Framework

## 1. Macro-to-Micro Evaluation Protocol
Analyze investment assets using the following top-down order of priority:

1. **Global Macro Indicators:** 
   * US Federal Reserve interest rate trajectory and commentary.
   * US 10-Year Treasury Yields and Dollar Index (DXY) directional trends.
   * Brent Crude Oil spot price movement (critical impact on Indian inflation).

2. **Indian Macroeconomic Health:**
   * RBI Monetary Policy decisions and repo rate changes.
   * Monthly GST collections, Manufacturing/Services PMI, and Credit Growth.
   * Government Capital Expenditure (Capex) rollouts across core industries.

3. **Sectoral Analysis:**
   * Identify structural tailwinds vs cyclical/seasonal economic peaks.
   * Review industrial growth data (e.g., Auto sales, Electronic Manufacturing order books).

## 2. Bottom-Up Fundamental Analysis Rules
When analyzing individual company equities, crosscheck against these rigid performance filters:

*   **Capital Efficiency:** 5-year average ROCE and ROE must be above 15%.
*   **Leverage Profile:** Debt-to-Equity ratio must be under 1.0 (prefer 0 for mid/small caps).
*   **Solvency:** Interest Coverage Ratio must be greater than 4.
*   **Cash Flow Quality:** Operating Cash Flow (OCF) must closely track Net Profit. Free Cash Flow (FCF) must be positive.
*   **Valuation Check:** Current P/E and EV/EBITDA compared against historical 5-year median bands.

## 3. Technical Chart Analysis Anchor Points
Before executing buy or sell trades, utilize technical analysis to time market entries:

*   **Trend Hierarchy:** Price position relative to the 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Averages (SMA).
*   **Market Structure:** Validation of higher-high and higher-low sequences on weekly/daily charts.
*   **Volume Profile:** Evidence of institutional accumulation (surging volume on green days, drying volume on pullbacks).
