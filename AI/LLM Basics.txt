-----------------------------------Basic--------------------------------------------
- LLMs are AI systems that are trained on vast amount of data to understand and generate human like language. 

- Transformers - transformers architecture are the backbones of all LLMs. lets understand LLMs each word in input text at the same time in parallel - THIS MECHANISM IS CALLED AS SELF-ATTENTION. 

- GPT - Generative pre-trained transformer. GPTs generates the text by predicting the next word/token - known as AUTOREGRESSION - NEXT TOKEN PREDICTION.
