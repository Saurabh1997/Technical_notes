-----------------------------------Basic--------------------------------------------
- LLMs are AI systems that are trained on vast amount of data to understand and generate human like language. 

- Transformers - transformers architecture are the backbones of all LLMs. lets understand LLMs each word in input text at the same time in parallel - THIS MECHANISM IS CALLED AS SELF-ATTENTION. 

- GPT - Generative pre-trained transformer. GPTs generates the text by predicting the next word/token - known as AUTOREGRESSION - NEXT TOKEN PREDICTION. 

-  

-----------------------------------------LLLMS WORKING--------------------------------------------------------------------------
It doesn't read full sentence - it will convert each word into Tokens (tokenization) and then each token is converted into Vectors (list of numbers) which captures meanings.
- this list of number - vectors - uses word embeddings to understand the relationship between this words (for example - cat is related to animal but not to car - it finds out the relationship between 2 words). 

- Now order matters in languages. for this Models use Positional encoding to identify how sentences should be formed from words. NOW THE DATA IS READY TO BE PROCESSED - THE ROLE OF TRANSFORMERS COMES HERE - Transformer ARCHITECTURE WHICH MIGHT HAVE MULTIPLE LAYERS (MORE THAN 100 SOMETIMES) - EACH LAYER PROCESSES THE INPUT TO UNDERSTAND AND REFINE. 

--- Self-Attention - it sees which words matter the most. For example - Explain photosynthesis. it will focus on photosynthesis and then will explain it using the words.
